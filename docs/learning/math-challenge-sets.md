# Math challenge sets — pilot candidates

Selected 5 September 2026 after Jenna reported that Brody found the answers
immediately and the repetition too easy. Jenna requested all three mission
designs for different challenges, longer challenges, and more sets. This narrow
extension adds counting, picture addition, and missing-amount facts within 10.
It does not approve new engines, AR/PW work, or later-grade content.

| Set | Counting | Picture addition | Missing amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trail Team | 1–10 | Positive groups totaling 2–5 | Make 5 |
| River Rescue | 6–10 | Positive groups totaling 6–10 | Make 10 |
| Beacon Champions | 6–10 | Groups of at least 2, totaling 6–10 | Mixed targets 6–10, with at least 1 already present |

Beacon Champions keeps the larger counting range from River Rescue. Both
addition groups contain at least two objects, and missing-amount questions
start with a nonempty group so the target numeral alone is not the answer.

Each set supplies three stages for a 24-success journey, with the existing
eight-success save and celebration at each stage. Continuing remains optional;
this data change does not alter the checkpoint, reward, or adaptive-selection
rules. A journey samples its set; 24 successes do not mean every fact was seen.

The default set preserves every existing item and its order. A fact keeps the
same ID wherever it appears, so changing sets does not erase its practice
history or create a second mastery record. Addition keeps both positive
addends. Trail Team and River Rescue missing-amount items may start with zero
objects; Beacon Champions starts with at least one. All missing-amount items
need at least one more. Three answer choices remain within 0–10.

These are candidates for another adult-led pilot, not validated difficulty
levels or evidence of mastery. Observe the first response, counting support,
retries, engagement, and device access separately. More steps alone do not
establish learning. The laptop findings do not replace the pending physical
Android observations or the broader validation gate.

Compiled from the app exports pasted 1–4 September 2026. This is the source of truth for
what the game has to cover; PLAN.md decides what gets built and when.

Grade labels: Jenna identified the PK and K tiers. Grade 1–5 labels are inferred from
content (standard scope-and-sequence) — the exports themselves didn't name grades.
Numbers in parentheses are skill counts as exported. Percentages are Brody's progress
at export time; anything unmarked is not started.

Changes: isolated the initial consonant (M: 0.24–0.33 seconds; N: 0.30–0.45
seconds; S: 0.24–0.38
seconds), repeated it with 10ms crossfades to sustain 0.6 seconds, normalized
peak amplitude to 0.6, applied 30ms edge fades, and converted to mono 44.1kHz
16-bit PCM WAV. Vowel portions of the source recordings are excluded. These
adaptations carry the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license. Adult auditory validation is
recorded in `docs/qa/pilot-voice-correction.md`.
